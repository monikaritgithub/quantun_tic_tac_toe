Allan Goff's Quantum Tic-Tac-Toe Rules

Invented by physicist Dr. Allan Goff (*American Journal of Physics*, 2006), Quantum Tic-Tac-Toe models quantum mechanics using pure graph theory.

1. Superposition (Two Spooky Marks Per Turn)

Each turn places two marks in two different open squares (e.g. X1 in Square 1 and 5). Marks exist in equal superposition until measured.

2. Entanglement (Shared Squares)

Multiple marks can share squares. When marks share squares, their eventual outcomes become entangled dependencies.

3. Cyclic Entanglement (Measurement Trigger)

When moves complete a closed loop, the system reaches the measurement threshold. An immediate collapse is triggered!

4. Opponent Chooses Collapse

The player who did not create the cycle chooses which of the two binary configurations solidifies into classical reality.

5. Domino Cascade

Resolving the cycle forces dependent stems to collapse into their only open squares in a deterministic chain reaction.

6. Allan Goff's Subscript Tiebreaker Rule

If both players complete 3-in-a-row simultaneously, the player whose winning line has the smaller maximum move subscript completed their line earlier and is awarded 1.0 point (Winner); the opponent gets 0.5 points.
