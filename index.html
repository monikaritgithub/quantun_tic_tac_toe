How to Play Quantum Tic-Tac-Toe

Quantum Tic-Tac-Toe generalizes classic Tic-Tac-Toe using real Qiskit quantum computing mechanics:

  • Superposition (|+⟩): All 9 squares start in equal superposition $|+\rangle = (|0\rangle + |1\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$. Neither player owns any tile initially (50% O, 50% X).
  • Move 'O' (Ry -π/2): Rotates the qubit to state $|0\rangle$ (North pole of Bloch sphere), claiming the square for Player O.
  • Move 'X' (Ry +π/2): Rotates the qubit to state $|1\rangle$ (South pole of Bloch sphere), claiming the square for Player X.
  • NOT Gate (Pauli-X): Inverts an owned square ($|0\rangle \leftrightarrow |1\rangle$). If applied to an unowned superposition square, $X|+\rangle = |+\rangle$ (leaves it neutral!).
  • SWAP Gate: Select two squares to exchange their quantum states, transposing strategic positions across the board.
  • Tug-of-War / Interference: Applying opposite rotations to the same square cancels out: $R_y(\pi/2) \cdot R_y(-\pi/2) = I$, restoring the neutral superposition state.
  • Measure: Collapses all 9 qubits into definite classical outcomes. Outcome '0' gives Player O, and '1' gives Player X. The player with the most completed lines wins! Both players can even win simultaneously!
